🔒 Privacy Policy

How Scroll DNA handles your data

1. Information We Collect

When you use Scroll DNA, we collect the following information:

  • Social Media Screenshots: Images of your Instagram or TikTok feed that you voluntarily upload for AI analysis.
  • Profile Pictures: Optional profile images you upload to appear on your generated trading card.
  • Username/Handle: Your social media identifier used to personalize your card.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics about how you interact with our service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your uploaded images are processed in real-time by our AI system to generate your personalized trading card. Specifically:

  • Images are analyzed for content patterns, aesthetic qualities, and engagement metrics.
  • After analysis, your original screenshots are automatically discarded from our servers.
  • We do not store, share, or sell your social media content to third parties.
  • Generated card data (stats, rarity, personality name) is stored to display your collection.

3. AI Processing & Third Parties

We use Advanced AI Vision to analyze your uploaded images. This processing occurs on secure, encrypted servers. Our AI does not retain your images after processing is complete.

We also use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies to:

  • Maintain your session and collection data.
  • Remember your preferences (sound settings, filters).
  • Serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transfers.
  • Automatic deletion of uploaded images after processing.
  • No storage of sensitive social media credentials.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your generated cards and associated data.
  • Opt out of data collection by discontinuing use of the service.
  • Contact us with privacy concerns (see our Contact page).

7. Children's Privacy

Scroll DNA is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

⬡ About Scroll DNA

AI-Powered Social Trading Cards · Built with AI

What is Scroll DNA?

Scroll DNA is an AI-powered social identity platform that transforms your Instagram and TikTok presence into collectible trading cards. Our proprietary system analyzes your content, personality, and aesthetic to generate a unique "social DNA" card complete with rarity tiers, stats, and a custom personality archetype.

Since our launch, over 2.4 million cards have been minted by 847,000+ collectors worldwide. We've trended as high as #2 on TikTok and continue to grow as the premier destination for social media trading card generation.

How It Works

Our technology stack combines computer vision, natural language processing, and creative AI to deliver results that feel personal and accurate:

  • Upload: Share screenshots of your social media feed and profile picture.
  • Analyze: Advanced AI Vision scans your content for patterns, aesthetics, engagement signals, and personality markers.
  • Generate: Our system assigns you a rarity (Common, Rare, Epic, or Legendary), six core stats, a grade, and a unique personality name.
  • Collect & Share: Save your card to your collection, download it, or share it directly to social media.

The Rarity System

Every card is assigned a rarity tier based on the depth and quality of your social media presence:

  • Common (70%): The everyday scroller. Still a valued part of the collection.
  • Rare (22%): Above-average energy. Your feed stands out from the crowd.
  • Epic (7%): Exceptional presence. Collectors actively seek these cards.
  • Legendary (1%): Once-in-a-generation social DNA. Only 156 have ever been minted.

Our Technology

Scroll DNA is built on cutting-edge AI infrastructure:

  • AI Vision Engine: Powers the image analysis and personality detection.
  • Privacy-First Design: Images are analyzed in real-time and immediately discarded. We never store your feed.
  • Responsive Web App: Works seamlessly on mobile and desktop without requiring an app download.
  • Real-Time Processing: Most cards generate in under 2 minutes.

Why Cards Go Viral

Scroll DNA cards are designed to be shared. The combination of personalized AI analysis, collectible rarity mechanics, and visually striking card designs creates natural viral potential. Users share their cards to compare rarities, trade stats, and discover their "social DNA" — creating organic growth loops across TikTok, Instagram, and X.
